Our Story

About TrendyB

TrendyB is a Kenyan-born womenswear brand that empowers women to dress boldly, confidently, and intentionally without compromising professionalism at the same time look powerful, polished and unapologetically stylish. Our collections are designed to celebrate individuality, self-expression, and the art of dressing well. Each piece is thoughtfully crafted with attention to detail, quality fabrics, and a commitment to sustainability.

Where it started

What began as a single rack of hand-finished pieces has grown into a fashion house that partners with independent designers across Nairobi each with their own name, story, and signature style.

How we work

Every drop is limited and handmade cut, beaded, sewn, or welded by someone whose name is on the tag. When a piece sells out, it's gone; we don't restock mass runs.
